## What is this study about?

I am developing a web-based platform that connects disaster victims, donors, and volunteers to coordinate aid requests and deliveries in Sri Lanka, as part of my BSc dissertation. This session asks you to try out the system by completing a few short tasks, then share your feedback on how easy or difficult it was to use.

## What will I be asked to do?

You'll be asked to complete a small set of tasks in the web application, acting as one of the system's user roles (a disaster victim, a donor, a volunteer, or an admin — you'll be told which). Afterwards, you'll fill in a short questionnaire about your experience and answer a few open-ended questions.

The whole session takes about **20-30 minutes**.

## Is this real data?

**No.** You will use a test account with made-up details — never your real name, address, national ID, or any other personal information. The system itself (outside this test) is a research prototype, not a live production service handling real disaster data.

## Do I have to take part?

No — participation is entirely voluntary. You can stop at any point, skip any task or question, and withdraw from the study at any time without giving a reason and without any consequence. If you withdraw, any data collected from your session will be deleted on request.

## Will my data be recorded?

- Your **task performance** (which tasks you completed, how long they took, and any difficulties observed) and your **questionnaire answers** will be recorded and used only for this dissertation's evaluation chapter.
- [ ] I agree to my session being **screen-recorded** for the researcher's later review (optional — leave unchecked to decline; you can still take part either way).
- All data will be **anonymized** — you will not be named or otherwise identified in the dissertation. Data will be stored securely and only accessible to the researcher and their supervisor, and will be deleted after the dissertation is marked (or per your institution's data retention policy).

## Are there any risks?

No known risks beyond the normal experience of using a website — there is no physical, financial, or significant psychological risk involved. If any task or question makes you uncomfortable, you're free to skip it or stop the session.
